## Problem Statement

Across Sub-Saharan Africa, paper-based polling unit result sheets, such as Nigeria's Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) Form EC8A and Kenya's Form 34A, remain the legal foundation of democratic elections. While biometric accreditation tablets (BVAS) capture and upload smartphone photographs of handwritten Form EC8A sheets to the public INEC Result Viewing Portal (IReV), auditing hundreds of thousands of uploaded sheets in real time remains an unsolved computer vision and statutory verification challenge. Existing commercial Optical Character Recognition (OCR) engines fail catastrophically on full-page photographed tally sheets due to camera skew, page folds, uneven illumination, and grid-line collisions, while frontier proprietary Vision-Language Models (VLMs) impose prohibitive pay-as-you-go API costs, aggressive rate limits, and severe prior-driven hallucination risks when reading faint or ambiguous ballpoint-pen numerals. Furthermore, literal single-pass statutory checks under Section 51(2) of Nigeria's Electoral Act 2022 conflate benign Presiding Officer arithmetic mistakes on ballot accounting headers with fraudulent over-voting, risking the wrongful disenfranchisement of entire polling units.

## Core Hypothesis

We hypothesize that an open-weight 7-billion-parameter Vision-Language Model (`Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct`) quantized to 4-bit NormalFloat (`NF4`) and deployed on zero-cost commodity cloud hardware (a single NVIDIA T4 GPU with 16 GB VRAM) can achieve $>98\%$ exact handwritten digit extraction accuracy (`Exact Match (%)`) across multi-party Form EC8A tables when coupled with a deterministic **Two-Tier Statutory Mathematical Reconciliation Gate**. Specifically, we propose that:

1. **Zero-Shot Tabular Vision Extraction:** Unlike traditional cropped-line OCR models (`TrOCR`, `Tesseract`, `PP-StructureV3`) that collapse on skewed or warped tables, `Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct`'s native dynamic-resolution vision transformer (`NaViT`) can jointly parse spatial row headers (15 political party acronyms: `A`, `AA`, `AAC`, `ADC`, `ADP`, `APC`, `APGA`, `APM`, `APP`, `BP`, `NNPP`, `PRP`, `SDP`, `YPP`, `ZLP`) and handwritten vote tallies directly from full-page IReV camera photographs without custom bounding-box object detectors.
2. **Self-Verifying Error Detection via Section 64(4):** Because autoregressive VLM errors on tabular tally sheets are dominated by sparse-row zero-bias ($1 \to 0$) and grid-line leading-digit truncation ($141 \to 41$), enforcing the Electoral Act Section 64(4) Collation Sum Invariant ($\sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} V_p = V_{\text{valid}}$) deterministically intercepts $>95\%$ of all VLM transcription errors without human ground truth.
3. **Clerical-Slip Disentanglement under Section 51(2):** By cross-checking the literal Section 51(2) inequality ($V_{\text{cast}} \le A_{\text{BVAS}}$) against reconciled ballot conservation ($V_{\text{valid}} + V_{\text{rejected}} \le A_{\text{BVAS}}$ and $\sum_{p} V_p = V_{\text{valid}}$), an automated audit engine can separate harmless Presiding Officer subtraction slips from genuine over-voting anomalies.

## Proposed Methodology (Detailed Technical Approach)

We introduce **EC8A-Sentinel**, an open-weight, sovereign forensic election audit framework evaluated on the **Osun-EC8A-250** benchmark dataset across three Local Government Areas (`ATAKUMOSA EAST`, `ATAKUMOSA WEST`, and `AYEDAADE`), encompassing 250 authentic IReV Form EC8A photographs and 3,750 handwritten party tally cells. The framework consists of three integrated modules:

1. **4-Bit Quantized Dynamic-Resolution Vision Extraction (`Qwen2.5-VL-7B-NF4`):**
   Each raw Form EC8A camera photograph $I \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times 3}$ (3–4 MB high-resolution smartphone capture streamed from the INEC IReV portal archive) is processed via `Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct` quantized to 4-bit NormalFloat (`NF4`) with `float16` compute precision. Using a schema-constrained zero-shot extraction prompt, the model extracts the tuple $(\hat{\mathbf{v}}, \hat{A}_{\text{BVAS}}, \hat{V}_{\text{valid}}, \hat{V}_{\text{rejected}}, \hat{V}_{\text{cast}})$, where $\hat{\mathbf{v}} = (\hat{v}_1, \dots, \hat{v}_{15}) \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}^{15}$ denotes the numeric vote tallies across all 15 political parties.

2. **Statutory Collation Invariant Solver (Electoral Act Section 64(4)):**
   To intercept VLM hallucinations and omission errors without ground-truth transcripts, the solver evaluates the internal collation balance indicator $B_{64} \in \{0, 1\}$:
   $$B_{64} = \mathbb{I}\!\left(\sum_{p=1}^{15} \hat{v}_p = \hat{V}_{\text{valid}}\right)$$
   Any sheet with $B_{64} = 0$ (`Sec64 Balanced (%)` failure) is automatically flagged and routed to the human exception queue, preventing unverified OCR errors from corrupting aggregate constituency totals.

3. **Two-Tier Over-Voting & Clerical-Slip Disentanglement Solver (Electoral Act Section 51(2)):**
   Under literal Section 51(2) of the Nigerian Electoral Act 2022, a polling unit triggers a primary violation flag $F_{51}^{\text{raw}} = \mathbb{I}(\hat{V}_{\text{cast}} > \hat{A}_{\text{BVAS}})$. However, presiding officers frequently compute "Total Ballot Papers Used" ($\hat{V}_{\text{cast}}$) by erroneously subtracting unused serial numbers from issued booklet ranges rather than summing valid and rejected ballots. We define the **Reconciled Ballot Cast** $\tilde{V}_{\text{cast}} = \hat{V}_{\text{valid}} + \hat{V}_{\text{rejected}}$ and partition all $F_{51}^{\text{raw}} = 1$ polling units into two disjoint forensic classes:
   - **Class I (Benign Clerical Slip):** $F_{51}^{\text{raw}} = 1 \;\wedge\; B_{64} = 1 \;\wedge\; (\tilde{V}_{\text{cast}} \le \hat{A}_{\text{BVAS}})$. Here, the candidate votes sum exactly to valid votes and the physical ballots counted in the box do not exceed biometric accreditation.
   - **Class II (Unresolved Over-Voting Anomaly):** $F_{51}^{\text{raw}} = 1 \;\wedge\; (\tilde{V}_{\text{cast}} > \hat{A}_{\text{BVAS}} \;\lor\; B_{64} = 0)$.
